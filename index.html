What is YDST?

The YourDegree Scholarship Test is a free online aptitude test. Score 170/200 or above and you get 50% off your first-semester tuition at any university on YourDegree.

Which universities can I use it at?

Every university listed on YourDegree, including D.Y. Patil, Jain, Kurukshetra, Andhra, Bharathidasan, KL, Vivekananda Global, Centurion and Noida International University.

Is the test free?

Yes. There is no fee to register or take the test.

When can I take the test?

Anytime. The test is live 24×7. You get one attempt per calendar month.

What do I need?

A laptop or desktop with a webcam and microphone, a stable internet connection, and 150 uninterrupted minutes. The test runs in full screen and is AI-proctored.

Is there negative marking?

No. Each correct answer gets 1 mark and wrong answers cost nothing.

What if I score below 170?

You can retake next month with a new paper. You can also apply to your program anytime, and a counsellor will help with your options.

Can I combine it with other offers?

No. The YDST scholarship can't be combined with any other discount, offer or scholarship.
